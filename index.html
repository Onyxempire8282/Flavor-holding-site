About Flavor Holdings

Built for the independent edge.

Flavor Holdings LLC is a licensed auto damage appraisal firm operating remotely across multiple markets. We partner with TPAs, insurance carriers, and self-insured entities to provide fast, accurate vehicle inspections — managed from assignment through delivery.

Our operations run on Claim Cipher, a proprietary platform built specifically for independent appraisal firms. Every claim is dispatched, tracked, and delivered with the transparency your team requires.
